# Roadmap and Milestones

*What gets made, in what order, and how progress is judged*

This master document is the reference, and what people read is built from it: a concept note, a documentary site, and a pitch deck. [Established] (D-082) The steps below come in this order, with the documentary site and the survey site built side by side. [Established] Institutional progress is tracked separately, on a seven-stage ladder where each stage needs its own evidence. [Established]

## Outputs and steps

1. **Master document.** This document, the reference for everything after it. Decisions live in its register.
2. **Concept note.** A short note for the first approach to OVPSAS, built from this document (D-083) and tried first on readers the author trusts (D-095).
3. **Scrollytelling documentary site.** Interactive, for students, faculty, staff, and administration. Readers choose topics, expand them, and see real VSU examples. It is built now, with placeholders where VSU evidence is still missing, filled in as field research reports. Administration and other interested parties see it early; students see it only after they have answered the survey (D-084).
4. **Student survey site.** Built alongside the documentary site, on a site of its own, so respondents don't read the proposal before answering (D-037, D-039).
5. **OVPSAS.** Two separate asks: permission to run the survey and the interviews, and interest in CAMPUS ([Chapter 3.4](03d-network-proposal.md); D-083).
6. **Field research.** The survey once permission is granted, the student interviews, and the Facebook landscape study ([Chapter 8.1](08a-facebook-study.md)). Which parts need VSU's permission or an ethics review is open (Q-51), and the protocol is published in [Chapter 8](08-evidence.md) before the survey opens (D-086).
7. **Pitch slides.** For OVPSAS first. Problem slides use only problems that clear the D-038 bars.
8. **Hop-It Alpha.** Follows its PRD: synthetic rehearsal, individual baseline, bundled cohort, evidence report, decision.

Field research feeds Chapters 3 and 8 and supplies the documentary site's and the pitch's real VSU examples. [Proposed]

## The milestone ladder

| Stage | What it means | Evidence that it happened |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recognition | A stakeholder understands the problem or concept | They engage with it in their own words; note whether personally or officially |
| Interest | A stakeholder sees potential relevance | A follow-up or request that comes from them |
| Alignment | Institutional stakeholders see a fit with a VSU need or direction | Stated by them, ideally in writing |
| Permission to prototype | Appropriate authority allows a bounded experiment | Written approval that names the scope |
| Pilot | Users or offices take part in structured testing under that permission | Dates, participants, and agreed measures |
| Collaboration | Responsibilities, scope, data access, or governance are formally set | A named counterpart and documented terms |
| Adoption | VSU formally decides to use or deploy it | An institutional decision |

Interest is not permission, permission is not a pilot, and a pilot is not adoption. v0.1 had an eighth stage, commercialization, which is gone because CAMPUS is not a commercial product (D-029). As of October 2026, no VSU office has reached any stage. [Established]

## Permissions

Some steps need a permission that is not a stage of support. Each is asked for and granted on its own, in writing, and none of them moves VSU up the ladder. [Established] (D-090)

| Permission | What it allows | Who grants it, as far as known | Status, October 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Research permission | Running the student survey and the interviews, with a campus ID or pass for the author if VSU requires one | Asked of OVPSAS first (D-083); the deciding office is open (Q-51) | Not yet asked |
| Ethics clearance | Research with people as participants | VSU's Research Ethics Board, through its Human Ethics Review Committee, if its review covers a study by an alumnus (E-051, Q-51) | Not yet asked |
| Access to VSU data or systems | Aggregate network figures (D-058), or a look inside VSU's existing systems | VSU ICT for network figures; otherwise each system's owner, not yet known (Q-10) | Not yet asked |

Informal advice and feedback from VSU people count as none of these. [Established] (D-090) Permission to prototype and the pilot are permissions too, and they stay on the ladder as stages of support (D-029). [Established]

## Environments

Work moves through environments in order, so a prototype is never mistaken for a service: local development, controlled prototype, demonstration, research test, institutional pilot (only with authorization), and production (only after adoption). [Established] (v0.1 §12.21)

## Change control

Changes are classed as minor, architectural, scope, hypothesis, institutional, or strategic, and anything above minor goes into the decision register before it appears in any document.
