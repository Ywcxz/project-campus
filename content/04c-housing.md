# Housing

*Verified listings for boarding houses and rentals near campus*

Housing lists boarding houses and rentals near the Main Campus for students who live off campus. University dormitories are not part of it: they stay with VSU's own housing information and the network's dormitory communities. [Established] (D-080) An earlier housing site was retired when Hop-It became the first product, and that decision stands; this entry returns to the same need as one product among several. [Established] (D-028, D-080)

## What a listing would show

- The property: type, rooms, monthly price range, utilities, and whether water and Wi-Fi are included. [Proposed]
- How far it is from campus, and the way there, from Wayfinder ([Chapter 4.4](04d-wayfinder.md)). [Proposed]
- House rules, such as curfews and visitors, as the landlord states them. [Proposed]
- When a room is free, confirmed by the landlord at a set interval, so old listings expire on their own. [Proposed]
- Reviews from verified members who lived there, after their stay. [Proposed]

## How it links to the network

Housing shares the network's verified membership, so reviews come only from VSU people who rented there. [Proposed] Landlords are not members; how they are verified is open. [Unresolved] The network's dormitory communities would point to Housing for students moving off campus. [Proposed]
