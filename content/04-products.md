# Products

*Hop-It first, and the products that could follow it*

The campus intranet ([Chapter 2](02-intranet.md)) and the social/academic network ([Chapter 3](03-social-network.md)) are the backbone and the centerpiece of CAMPUS. This chapter collects the products: separate tools that each do one job on campus and are useful on their own. [Established] (D-077) Hop-It is the first, and the only one with a specification. [Established] (D-026) The others are short entries, written so they can be tested before anything is designed in detail. [Established] (D-077)

## The products at a glance

| Product | Its job | Stage | What it builds on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Hop-It](04a-hop-it.md) | Scheduled, pooled delivery on campus | Specified for Alpha [Established] | Nothing from CAMPUS, by design [Established] |

| [Digital twin](04e-digital-twin.md) | A living model of the campus for planning and facilities | Research only [Proposed] | Wayfinder's places, VSU's spatial data, and the intranet's later sensors [Proposed] |

## What every product shares

- **One job each.** A product is judged by whether it does its one job better than what people use now, not by how much of CAMPUS it uses. [Proposed]
- **Useful alone.** Each product works without the others. Where one builds on another, as Housing builds on Wayfinder's places, it falls back to something simpler when the other isn't there. [Proposed]
- **One identity, when there is one.** Once the network exists, the products share its verified membership, so a buyer, a tenant, and someone asking the way are the same member. Hop-It is the exception: it runs without any CAMPUS sign-on by design. [Established] (D-026)
- **The same seven questions.** Every entry answers what problem, who needs it, what evidence, its relation to VSU's systems, its stage, its privacy, governance, and cost, and how it could be disproved. [Established] (project rule 3)
- **Evidence before a chapter.** An entry grows into a full chapter only when field research supports the problem it names. [Proposed] (principle 1)

## How a new product gets an entry

Other ideas will come up. Each gets an entry here only when it names a problem people at VSU have, the people who have it, and how the idea could be shown wrong. [Proposed] (principle 5) An idea without those stays in conversation, or among the [later directions](05-later-directions.md) if it needs more than a product can carry. [Proposed] Every new entry is recorded in the [decision register](12-decisions.md). [Established]

## Which comes next

Hop-It comes first because it can be built without VSU's systems, as working proof that the project can ship. [Established] (D-026) Which product follows it is open. [Unresolved] (Q-48) Wayfinder's office guide needs the least new infrastructure, and the marketplace and Housing need the network's moderation and verified membership first. [Proposed] Field research decides among them, through the same bars the pitch uses (D-038). [Proposed]
