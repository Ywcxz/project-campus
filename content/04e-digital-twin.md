# Digital Twin

*A living model of the campus, built on the products before it*

A digital twin is an interactive model of the campus: its buildings, rooms, roads, and utilities, and what is happening in them. It moved here from the [later directions](05-later-directions.md) on 9 October 2026, and it keeps the conditions that held it back: it needs spatial data, operational integration, and infrastructure far beyond any first product. [Established] (D-077) It is listed among the products so that the path toward it is visible, and so that each step on that path is useful on its own. [Proposed]

## At a glance

| Question | Current answer |
| --- | --- |
| What problem? | Facilities, utilities, and space information sit in separate places, so planning, maintenance, and emergency response can't see the campus as a whole. [Unresolved] |
| Who needs it? | Facilities and planning staff, energy managers, and the disaster risk management office first; students later, through Wayfinder ([Chapter 4.4](04d-wayfinder.md)). [Proposed] |
| What evidence? | A light review of campus twins at Glasgow and Manchester ([Chapter 9](09-related-work.md)). [Established] Nothing at VSU. [Unresolved] |
| Relation to existing systems? | VSU has completed an inventory of roads, water, drainage, electrical systems, and GIS, and DIGITS envisions sensors for energy, water, and facilities. [Established] Any twin would be VSU's own, built on VSU's data; CAMPUS would not own it. [Proposed] |
| Stage? | Research only. [Proposed] A full twin is outside any first build. [Established] (v0.1 D-008) |
| Privacy, governance, and cost? | The highest infrastructure cost of any product. [Proposed] Occupancy data can reveal where people are, so it is counted, never tracked by person, and maps of utilities stay restricted. [Proposed] |
| How could it be disproved? | VSU's GIS and facility systems already meet planning needs; no office wants to maintain a twin; or its cost outweighs what VSU would gain. [Proposed] |

## A path, not a project

Each step stands on its own, and none commits VSU to the next. [Proposed]

1. **Places.** Wayfinder's buildings, rooms, and offices, with the names people use. [Proposed]
2. **VSU's spatial data.** The GIS inventory's layers, if VSU shares them. [Unresolved] (Q-49)
3. **Status offices already keep.** Room bookings, outages, and maintenance, linked where VSU permits. [Proposed]
4. **Sensors.** Energy, water, and occupancy readings carried on the campus network's later stage ([Chapter 2](02-intranet.md)). [Deferred]

## What waits

Three-dimensional models, simulation, and live sensor dashboards wait until the first three steps show an office would use them. [Deferred] In the light review, campus twins serve estates and energy teams first, and the student-facing use is closer to a guide to places, which Wayfinder already covers. [Proposed]
