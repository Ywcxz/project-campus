# Products

*Hop-It first, and the products that could follow it*

The campus intranet ([Chapter 2](02-intranet.md)) and the social/academic network ([Chapter 3](03-social-network.md)) are the backbone and the centerpiece of CAMPUS, and the digital twin ([Chapter 4](04-digital-twin.md)) is its long-term aspiration. This chapter collects the products: separate tools that each do one job on campus and are useful on their own. [Established] (D-077) Hop-It is the first, and the only one with a specification. [Established] (D-026) The others are short entries, written so they can be tested before anything is designed in detail. [Established] (D-077)

## The products at a glance

| Product | Its job | Stage | What it builds on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Hop-It](05a-hop-it.md) | Scheduled, pooled delivery on campus | Specified for Alpha [Established] | Nothing from CAMPUS, by design [Established] |
| [Marketplace](05b-marketplace.md) | Buying and selling between verified VSU members | Later [Proposed] | The network's verified membership and moderators [Proposed] |
| [Housing](05c-housing.md) | Verified listings for boarding houses and rentals near campus | Later [Proposed] | Verified membership; the digital twin's places for distance and routes [Proposed] |

## What every product shares

- **One job each.** A product is judged by whether it does its one job better than what people use now, not by how much of CAMPUS it uses. [Proposed]
- **Useful alone.** Each product works without the others. Where one builds on another, as Housing builds on the digital twin's places ([Chapter 4](04-digital-twin.md)), it falls back to something simpler when the other isn't there. [Proposed]
- **One identity, when there is one.** Once the network exists, the products share its verified membership, so a buyer and a tenant are the same member. Hop-It is the exception: it runs without any CAMPUS sign-on by design. [Established] (D-026)
- **The same seven questions.** Every entry answers what problem, who needs it, what evidence, its relation to VSU's systems, its stage, its privacy, governance, and cost, and how it could be disproved. [Established] (project rule 3)
- **Evidence before a chapter.** An entry grows into a full chapter only when field research supports the problem it names. [Proposed] (principle 1)

## How a new product gets an entry

Other ideas will come up. Each gets an entry here only when it names a problem people at VSU have, the people who have it, and how the idea could be shown wrong. [Proposed] (principle 5) An idea without those stays in conversation, or among the [later directions](06-later-directions.md) if it needs more than a product can carry. [Proposed] Every new entry is recorded in the [decision register](13-decisions.md). [Established]

## Which comes next

Hop-It comes first because it can be built without VSU's systems, as working proof that the project can ship. [Established] (D-026) Which product follows it is open. [Unresolved] (Q-48) The marketplace and Housing both need the network's moderation and verified membership first, while the digital twin's first step, places and the office guide, needs the least new infrastructure of anything after Hop-It. [Proposed] Field research decides among them, through the same bars the pitch uses (D-038). [Proposed]
