# Proposing the Network to VSU

*Who to ask, what to ask for, and how to earn each step*

The network leads the first pitch, which goes to OVPSAS, the Office of the Vice President for Student Affairs and Services. [Established] (D-024) This chapter plans how to propose it: whom at VSU it concerns, how it serves OVPSAS's own mandate, what the university would gain and give, which asks come in which order, how a pilot would be set up, and which questions to expect. It follows the project's rule that credibility comes from evidence, never from claims. [Established] (project rule 7)

> **In short.** Propose the network as a way to deliver services OVPSAS already owes students, and as a memory that spares offices and seniors from answering the same questions every year, not as an app or a complaints channel. Ask in steps that match the milestone ladder: first, permission to run the survey and, separately, interest in CAMPUS; then, with evidence, one office's commitment to answer students; then a one-semester pilot in one community, judged against thresholds fixed in advance and compared with a well-run Facebook group. Bring evidence and a stop condition to every step, and a plan for VSU to own the network or for it to end cleanly. [Proposed]

## OVPSAS's own mandate

CHED's policies on student affairs and services, CMO No. 9, s. 2013, group the services into student welfare, student development, and institutional student programs, and VSU's accreditation evidence lists its basic services under those three groups. [Established] The network maps onto most of them. That is the strongest case for OVPSAS to sponsor it: the network would be a way to deliver services OVPSAS already provides, not a new mandate. [Proposed]

## What VSU gains, and what it gives

| VSU gains | VSU gives |
| --- | --- |
| Questions answered once, in public, instead of many times at the counter | Staff time to answer and confirm, within agreed times |
| Counts of what students ask about, by month and topic | A moderation owner and a network council |
| Notices that can be checked against one source | A privacy impact assessment and, at scale, registration of a new system |
| An answerable channel for student voice that feeds the listening sessions | Rules approved by the right bodies |
| Organization records that outlast their officers | Permission for VSU sign-in |
| Answers that keep helping after the students who gave them graduate | Volunteers and office review for a guide sprint before launch |
| A tested design, built from open components VSU could host | If a pilot succeeds, an owner, hosting, and a budget |

Every row is a proposal. [Proposed] No gain should be promised in money or hours until a pilot measures it. [Established] (project rule 7)

## The asks, in order

Each ask is a separate step on the milestone ladder and needs its own evidence. [Established] (D-029)

| Step | The ask | Evidence brought | What VSU gives | Ladder stage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Permission to run the student survey | The survey site and how it handles data | Permission | Permission, for research only |
| 2 | Interest in CAMPUS, asked separately | This document and the documentary site | A follow-up | Recognition, then interest |
| 3 | One office's commitment to answer students within an agreed time, on a channel it already uses | Survey and interview findings that clear the D-038 bars | Staff time for a set period | Alignment |
| 4 | Permission for a one-semester pilot in one community | The results of step 3, and the pilot proposal below | Written permission naming the scope | Permission to prototype |
| 5 | Running the pilot | Weekly measures against thresholds fixed in advance | Participation, a liaison, and a moderation owner | Pilot |
| 6 | A decision on ownership | The pilot's evidence report | Ownership, hosting, and staff, or a clean stop | Collaboration, then adoption |

Steps 1 and 2 stay separate so that permission to do research never depends on interest in the proposal. [Established] (project instructions v3) Step 3 tests the network's real ask, office responsiveness, before any software is involved. [Proposed] (D-068)

## A pilot proposal on one page

| Part | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Question | Does a governed campus network do campus tasks better than a well-run Facebook group, for comparable communities, with the same office commitment? |
| Community | One community chosen from the evidence (Q-34), such as first-year students of one faculty, the residents of a few dormitories, or a cluster of organizations; a matched community uses a Facebook group run under the same rules |
| Size and length | A few hundred members, for one semester, clear of exam weeks |
| Offices | At least one office, answering within an agreed time on both sides of the comparison |
| Memory | Seeded before launch with official information and guides from a short guide sprint by seniors, officers, and alumni (D-070) |
| Measures | The share of the community that joins; weekly active members after the first month; the share of questions answered, and confirmed by an office, within the agreed time; how often members find an existing answer instead of asking, and how often kept answers are read again; how many helpers carry the load; members' preference for each task at the end; whether members can tell an official notice from a rumor, and a current answer from an outdated one, in a short test; reports per hundred members and the time to resolve them; and office, helper, and moderator minutes per week |
| Thresholds | Fixed before any data is read, ideally agreed with OVPSAS (D-067, Q-35) |
| Governance | Pilot rules approved by OVPSAS and the student council; a named moderation owner; the two-key process if pseudonymous sections are tested |
| Privacy | A privacy impact assessment with VSU's data protection officer, consent from every member, only the data the measures need, and at the end the data handed to VSU or deleted |
| Exit | If the pilot stops, members receive their posts and the network closes on a set date |
| Cost | One small server and the author's time; office and moderator time measured |

Every row is a proposal. [Proposed] The comparison group would be run on Facebook by the office or the student council, and measured by hand and through members' surveys, never by tools that read Facebook, in keeping with project rule 8. [Proposed]

## Questions to expect

| Question | Short answer | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why not just use Facebook? | Students would keep Facebook for life beyond the campus. For campus tasks the network adds verified membership, visible authority, answers that are kept and dated, records that last, and rules VSU's community writes, and the pilot tests whether that matters against a well-run Facebook group. | [Chapter 2](02-social-network.md) |
| Why not just use Messenger group chats? | Keep them for coordinating. They bury answers within days, so each batch asks again; the network keeps the answers and links into the chats. | [Chapter 2](02-social-network.md) |
| Isn't this a complaints platform? | No. It is where questions get answered and knowledge is kept. Concerns are one part of it: moderators keep the rules, and offices decide what needs a response. | [Chapter 2.2](02b-network-design.md) |
| Who will write the guides, and keep them current? | Seniors, officers, and alumni in a guide sprint before launch; offices that confirm them; and a review by each community every semester. | [Chapter 2.2](02b-network-design.md) |
| Isn't this OneVSU's job? | Possibly in part. CAMPUS asks first what OneVSU will cover and designs to complement it, and VSU decides the relationship. | [Chapter 2.3](02c-network-fit.md) |
| Who moderates? | Moderators from each community, trained network moderators, and a council with students on it, with VSU's offices handling cases beyond the rules. | [Chapter 2.2](02b-network-design.md) |
| Will VSU be liable for what students post? | A host has duties under several laws, and a prompt notice-and-action process is how a host meets them; a legal review comes before any real users. | [Chapter 2.3](02c-network-fit.md) |
| Can students criticize VSU on a VSU network? | Yes. The rules protect speech on student welfare and public matters, and a pseudonym is revealed only for named violations, through a process students share in. | [Chapter 2.2](02b-network-design.md) |
| What will it cost? | A pilot needs one small server. At scale, servers are modest and people are the main cost, which the pilot measures. | [Chapter 2.3](02c-network-fit.md) |
| What if students don't use it? | Then the pilot says so, against thresholds fixed in advance, and it stops. | This chapter |
| What happens if the author leaves? | The network is built from open components and documented, and every pilot ends with VSU owning it or with a clean close. | [Chapter 2.3](02c-network-fit.md) |
| Does it replace VSUEE, the portal, or the helpdesk? | No. It links into them. | [Chapter 2.3](02c-network-fit.md) |
| What data does it collect? | Only what each feature needs, with education details shown only by choice, under VSU's privacy program; knowledge is kept, and conversation fades on a published schedule. | [Chapter 2.2](02b-network-design.md) |
| Can parents and applicants use it? | Later, through a public section answered by verified members. The pilot is for current college members. | [Chapter 2.2](02b-network-design.md) |
| Why trust one alumnus with this? | Not on trust: judge the working software, the field evidence, and the recorded decisions. | Project rule 7 |
| What do you need from us now? | Permission to run the survey. Everything else waits for evidence. | This chapter |

## How to frame it

- **A network with a memory, not a complaints channel.** Lead with what the network does for everyone, offices included: questions that reach the people who know, answers that stay found, and notices people can check. Student voice is one part of it, answered by offices under the rules of D-066. Pitched as a grievance platform, it would put the offices it needs on the defensive. [Proposed]
- **Described by what it does.** "Social network" may be heard as one more Facebook for VSU to moderate, and "university operating system" as a bid for the work DIGITS plans. Describing the network by its three jobs (routing, memory, and trust) avoids both. [Proposed]
- **Beside DIGITS, not inside or above it.** The network complements VSU's systems from the community side. Whether and how it relates to DIGITS is VSU's decision. [Established] (D-017)
- **Seniors and offices as winners, not only students.** An office that answers a question once in public, and a senior whose guide keeps helping after she graduates, gain as much as the student who asks. [Proposed]

## The story for the slides

The pitch slides come after field research ([Chapter 11](11-roadmap.md)). A possible sequence, each slide a proposal: [Proposed]

1. **A morning at VSU.** The 4:30 a.m. suspension and the first-year student's question, told from the student's side.
2. **What students told us.** Only problems that clear the D-038 bars, each with an interview quote, a survey number, and a real example. (D-036)
3. **Where VSU's conversation lives.** The Facebook study's counts by kind of channel, with no community group named. (D-044)
4. **Every batch starts over.** Only if the interviews and the survey support it: the same questions asked year after year, and answers lost within days. (D-038)
5. **What VSU already has.** Its systems, shown with respect, and the gap between them.
6. **The network in three jobs, kept by memory.** Reaching the people who know, answers someone stands behind, and talking across roles, and what it will never have.
7. **How it would be governed.** With the community, by VSU's own principles, with due process, and with moderation kept apart from VSU's responses.
8. **The test.** The pilot, its comparison, its thresholds, and its stop conditions.
9. **The ask.** One office's commitment, then one semester.
10. **Who is behind it.** Hop-It's working software, the field research, and the decision register.

The framing should be cultivation rather than construction: a community network is not installed but grown, with its members, in ground prepared for it. [Proposed] The builders of an institution-run network that outlasted its predecessor drew the same conclusion in 2010, calling gardening a better metaphor than architecture because the system keeps changing as people use it. [Established] Which image the slides use is still open. [Unresolved]

## What not to claim

- That students want the network, before the survey and the interviews say so. [Established] (project rule 7)
- That any VSU office supports, endorses, or has reviewed CAMPUS, beyond what the milestone ladder records. [Established]
- Anything about DIGITS's architecture, status, or plans beyond the Third SOUA. [Established]
- Savings of money or time, before a pilot measures them. [Established]
- That the network would end harassment or misinformation. It would give VSU and its community better ways to respond. [Proposed]
- That it would replace group chats or Facebook. It complements chats, and competes with Facebook only for campus tasks. [Proposed] (D-074)
- That it is a platform for VSU's operations. It is a community layer beside them. [Proposed] Its relation to DIGITS is VSU's to decide. [Established] (D-017)
- Any private conversation, or any stakeholder by name. [Established] (D-030)
